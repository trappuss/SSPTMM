# Workshop

## Home

![Workshop Home](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/02b-workshop-home.png)

The past week's new mods, mods from authors you follow, list tabs (Top Rated, Most Subscribed, Last
Updated, New) and content types with counts. **Browse ▾** and **Your Items ▾** under the banner open
the other Workshop pages.

## Browse

![Browse](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/03-browse.png)

- **Filters** in Steam's sidebar: SPT version (tick **+** to show mods for that release), Content
  Type, Special Filters (Featured, Created by Followed), tags (**+** to include, **-** to leave out:
  Fika compatible, Has dependencies, Has addons, Contains ads, Subscribed) and **Filter by Date**.
- **Sort** like Steam: Top Rated All Time (sp-mod.com endorsements), Most Recent, Last Updated,
  Total Unique Subscribers (downloads), Most Favorited.
- **Search** the title, the description or both (the gear beside the search box); **@name**
  searches by author.
- Every active filter is a chip on the results line - click one to remove it.
- **Per page**: 10, 15, 30, 50 or **Infinite** (more load as you scroll). Remembered per page.
- **Save as default** (under the gear) opens Browse with your filters next time.

![Browse menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/03b-browse-menu.png)

### On a card

- **Hover** for Steam's popup: pictures, teaser, dates, SPT version, tags.
- The **+** subscribes; the **magnifier** opens **Quick View** (arrow keys step through the list).
- A badge shows when you have it installed, it has an update, or it is disabled.
- **Right-click** for Open, Quick View, Subscribe, Download only, Add to Collection, Copy link, View
  mod page, the author's Workshop and Follow.

![Right-click menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/03d-browse-context-menu.png)

## Item page

![Item page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/04-item.png)

- **Description** exactly as its author wrote it on sp-mod.com - tabs, tables, code, warnings,
  animated GIFs and videos - with the pictures in a strip and a full-size viewer.
- **Comments** - sp-mod.com's own comments, inside the app (sign in once there to post).
- **Change Notes** - every version's notes. **Versions** - each version's SPT range (green when it
  runs on yours), size, downloads, Fika status and requirements, with **Install this version** /
  **Switch to this version**.
- The side panel: content type (a link to Browse), tags, licence, GUID, source code, VirusTotal
  scans, which SPT it runs on, size and dates.
- **Required Items** (ticked when installed), **Required By** and **More By** this author.
- sp-mod.com's notices are shown: the multiplayer-cheat warning and "may make permanent changes to
  your profile".
- Buttons: **Subscribe** / **Unsubscribe**, **Download only**, View mod page, Copy link, Add to
  Collection.

![Item page, lower](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/04b-item-scrolled.png)

Esc, the mouse's back button or Alt+Left closes item, author and collection pages in the order they
were opened.

## Authors

Click an author's name for their page: Workshop Items, Collections and Addons, followers and
member-since date. **Follow** them; **Authors you follow** (Your Items) lists them, and Home shows
what they posted recently. Follows are kept on this PC.
