# Collections

Collections are lists of mods: public ones from sp-mod.com, and your own.

## Public collections (Workshop > Browse ▾ > Collections: Most Recent)

![Collections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/05-collections-browse.png)

- Search them (by name or by who made them, or paste a link), filter by SPT version, sort them.
- A collection's page shows its description, each item with the author's note and the version it
  gets on your SPT, and how much of it you already have.

| A collection | Its items |
|---|---|
| ![A collection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/06-collection.png) | ![Its items](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/06b-collection-items.png) |

- **Subscribe to all** installs each item at its newest version for your SPT. A collection made for
  another SPT asks **For My SPT** or **For the Collection's SPT**. You see what will change first,
  with **Add Only** or **Overwrite My Subscriptions**.
- **Unsubscribe from all** sets the items aside (disables them) or removes them.
- **Save to Collection** makes your own copy. **Favorite** keeps it on Your Items > Favorites, which
  marks the ones that changed since you last looked.

## Your collections (the Collections tab)

![Your collections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/08-your-collections.png)

- **Create from installed mods** records what is enabled now, at the versions you have.
- **Add from code** / **Add from file** take a collection a friend shared.
- **Follow a shared file** follows a friend's collection in a shared folder (Dropbox, OneDrive,
  Google Drive, a network share) and keeps it up to date.
- **Add to Collection** on any mod (item page, Quick View, right-click) puts it in one of yours.

Open one for its page: **Manage in Collections** and **Share with friends**.

![Your collection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/08b-your-collection.png)

### Sharing

![Share with friends](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/18-share-collection.png)

- **Share code** - one line of text that fits in a Discord message.
- **Shared folder** - a file in a folder you both sync; your friends' copies follow it.

When a friend's collection changes, a notice says exactly what was added or removed and offers
**Sync** or **Later**. A share code you copy is noticed when you switch to the app.

## Manage collections

![Manage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/08c-collection-manage.png)

- **Capture** saves what is installed now; **Import** reads a list file.
- **Preview** shows what applying the collection would install, update, enable and disable;
  **Apply** does it. Mods you [pinned](Subscribed-Items#pinned-mods) are never disabled.
- Per mod: where it applies (Server, Client, Headless), and remove.
- **Add mods**, **Refresh versions**, **Export**, **Rename**, **Delete**.
- **Publish to this server** hands the list out to players joining your server through the Server
  Map mod (see [Tools](Tools)).

A copy of your SPT profiles is taken before a collection is applied - see
[Safety and backups](Safety-and-Backups).
