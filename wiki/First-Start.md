# First start

## 1. Point it at your SPT folder

1. Open **Options** - the gear at the top right.
2. Under **SPT Install Folder**, press **Browse** and pick your SPT **game** folder - the one with
   `EscapeFromTarkov.exe` in it, not the server folder inside it.
3. Press **Save**. The line underneath, and the window title, now show your SPT version.

![Options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/14-options.png)

## 2. Getting started

The top of **Workshop** shows three steps, ticked off from your actual install: your SPT folder is
set, a mod is installed, and a profile exists. It goes away once all three are done, or when you
press **Hide**.

![Getting started](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trappuss/SSPTMM/main/docs/images/02-workshop-home.png)

## 3. Subscribe to a mod

1. Open **Workshop**, then **Browse**. The SPT version filter is already set to yours.
2. Open a mod and press **Subscribe**. If it needs other mods, you are asked once whether to
   subscribe to them too.
3. Watch the downloads bar at the bottom of the window. Click it for the
   [Downloads](Subscribing-and-Downloads) page.

## 4. Play

On [Play](Play), press **Start server**, then **Open launcher**, pick your profile and start the
game.

## Already have mods installed?

You don't need to do anything. [Subscribed items](Subscribed-Items) lists every mod in your SPT
folder - ones you installed by hand too - and matches them to sp-mod.com so updates work.
